Requirements:

* Use single-agent from above
* Use LangGraph only for orchestration.
* Start with a `user_request`.
* Send the `user_request` in parallel to `agent1` and `agent3`.
* `agent1` processes the request and passes its output to `agent2`.
* `agent2` receives both the original `user_request` and the output from `agent1`, then produces its own analysis.
* `agent3` independently processes the original `user_request`.
* The `summary_agent` receives the outputs from `agent2` and `agent3` only.
* The `summary_agent` combines those two results and produces the final response.
* `agent1` and `agent3` should run in parallel where supported by LangGraph.
* `agent2` must wait for `agent1`.
* `summary_agent` must wait for both `agent2` and `agent3`.
