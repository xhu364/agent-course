Company Policy Handbook

Company Name: TechNova Solutions
Document Version: 3.2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Human Resources Department

==================================================

1. Introduction

This employee handbook defines the policies, procedures, and guidelines that all employees of TechNova Solutions must follow.

The purpose of these policies is to create a safe, productive, and respectful workplace while ensuring consistent decision-making across all departments.

All employees are expected to read, understand, and comply with these policies. Failure to follow company policies may result in disciplinary action.

==================================================

2. Working Hours and Attendance Policy

Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time.

Employees are expected to be available during core business hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM for collaboration with team members.

Employees who are unable to attend work must notify their manager as soon as possible.

Attendance issues include:

- Repeated late arrivals
- Missing scheduled meetings without notice
- Failure to communicate absences
- Leaving work early without approval

Managers are responsible for monitoring attendance patterns and discussing repeated attendance problems with employees.

==================================================

3. Remote Work Policy

TechNova Solutions supports flexible remote work arrangements.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Remote employees must:

- Maintain a reliable internet connection
- Protect confidential company information
- Attend required meetings
- Remain reachable during working hours

Employees working remotely must use company-approved devices and security tools.

Remote work privileges may be revoked if productivity, communication, or security requirements are not maintained.

==================================================

4. Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Employees receive paid time off based on their employment level and years of service.

Full-time employees receive:

Years of Service:
0-2 years: 15 PTO days per year
3-5 years: 20 PTO days per year
More than 5 years: 25 PTO days per year

Employees should request PTO at least two weeks in advance whenever possible.

Managers may deny PTO requests during critical business periods if staffing requirements cannot be met.

Unused PTO rollover is limited to 10 days per calendar year.

==================================================

5. Sick Leave Policy

Employees may use sick leave for:

- Personal illness
- Medical appointments
- Caring for immediate family members
- Health-related emergencies

Employees should notify their manager before the start of their scheduled workday when taking sick leave.

For absences longer than five consecutive business days, employees may be required to provide medical documentation.

==================================================

6. Information Security Policy

Protecting company information is a critical responsibility of every employee.

Employees must:

- Use strong passwords
- Enable multi-factor authentication
- Lock computers when away from their desks
- Report security incidents immediately

Employees must not:

- Share passwords
- Install unauthorized software
- Transfer confidential data to personal accounts
- Store company data on unapproved cloud services

Security violations may result in termination of employment.

==================================================

7. Password Policy

All employees must follow these password requirements:

- Passwords must contain at least 12 characters
- Passwords must include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Passwords must include numbers or special characters
- Passwords cannot be reused from previous passwords

Passwords must be changed every 180 days.

Employees should use the company password manager to store credentials securely.

==================================================

8. Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Eligible expenses include:

- Business travel
- Customer meetings
- Training programs
- Required work equipment

Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of the expense date.

Each expense report must include:

- Date of expense
- Business purpose
- Amount spent
- Receipt documentation

Expenses over $1,000 require manager approval before purchase.

==================================================

9. Travel Policy

Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking.

Employees should select reasonable travel options that balance cost, convenience, and business needs.

Approved travel expenses include:

- Economy-class airfare
- Hotel accommodations
- Ground transportation
- Meals during business travel

Employees may not upgrade flights or hotels without prior approval.

==================================================

10. Code of Conduct Policy

Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times.

Prohibited behaviors include:

- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Workplace violence
- Retaliation against employees reporting concerns

Employees should report violations to:

- Their manager
- Human Resources
- The confidential ethics hotline

All reports will be reviewed according to company investigation procedures.

==================================================

11. Performance Review Policy

Employees participate in formal performance reviews twice per year.

Performance reviews evaluate:

- Technical skills
- Quality of work
- Collaboration
- Communication
- Achievement of goals

Managers provide feedback and create development plans when improvement opportunities are identified.

Employees are encouraged to discuss career goals with their managers.

==================================================

12. Employee Benefits Policy

Eligible employees may receive:

- Health insurance
- Dental insurance
- Vision insurance
- Retirement plan contributions
- Professional development benefits

Benefits eligibility depends on employment status and enrollment deadlines.

Employees should contact Human Resources for questions regarding benefits.

==================================================

13. Termination Policy

Employment may end due to:

- Employee resignation
- Performance issues
- Policy violations
- Business restructuring

Employees leaving the company must:

- Return company equipment
- Transfer knowledge to team members
- Remove company data from personal devices
- Complete exit procedures

Final compensation will be processed according to applicable laws and company procedures.

==================================================

14. Policy Updates

The company may modify policies as business needs change.

Employees will be notified of significant policy updates through official communication channels.

The latest version of this handbook is maintained by the Human Resources Department.
